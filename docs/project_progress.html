2. Training Sample Generation for Agriculture Land Probability Classification

  1. Create a binary mask of all the agricultural lands from 2000-2022 (note that the land cover dataset for 2012 is not available).
    Status: ✅ Completed
    Notes: Used FRTC land cover dataset for identifying stable agriculture lands.

  2. Identify the stable agricultural areas by finding the intersection of all the binary masks. Status: ✅ Completed
    Notes: Intersected the annual agricultural masks to isolate pixels consistently classified as agriculture across all available years (2000-2022).

  3. Remove the small pixel clusters, field boundaries using the morphological operations.
    Status: ✅ Completed
    Notes: Applied a 2-pixel erosion to eliminate boundary/edge effects, and filtered out isolated pixel clusters smaller than 10 pixels (~0.9 ha) to guarantee sample purity.

  4. Convert the final stable agricultural land pixels to points
    Status: ✅ Completed
    Notes: Extracted an initial pool of 100,000 random sample points from within the pure, stable agricultural mask.

  5. Spatial thinning of the points
    Status: ✅ Completed
    Notes: Applied a spatial filter enforcing a 2,000-meter minimum distance between points to mitigate spatial autocorrelation and ensure broad geographic representation.

  6. Final check using a phenology profile of the points to ensure they strictly exhibit agricultural characteristics (sharp increase and decrease in NDVI).
    Status: ⚙️ In Progress | Estimated Completion: 02-04-2026 (2-3 Days)
    Notes: Performing multi-temporal phenological filtering on training samples to ensure they consistently exhibit active agricultural signatures (e.g., high NDVI amplitude indicating plow/harvest cycles)

  7. Repeat the same for the stable non-agricultural lands.
    Status: ⬜ Not Started
    Notes: Will perform stratified sampling using the 2022 land cover dataset to ensure the model learns the spectral signatures of all non-cropland classes. Dominant land covers (forest, rangeland, bare soil/sand, water, and built-up areas) will be proportionally represented to minimize confusion errors.
